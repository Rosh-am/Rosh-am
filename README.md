## `02 / FEATURED — B.TECH CAPSTONE`

### ◈ Intelligent Water Quality Prediction & Explainable Risk Analysis Platform
An end-to-end decision-support platform that predicts the **CCME Water Quality Index (WQI)** and its category from river water-quality measurements, **explains every prediction with SHAP**, and turns guideline exceedances into prioritised, rule-based recommendations — all served through a live **Streamlit** dashboard.

- Compares **CatBoost** and **XGBoost** across baseline and imputed datasets (MAE, RMSE, R²)
- Global and per-prediction **SHAP** explanations
- Rule-based recommendation engine for prioritised corrective actions
- Pipeline: data → quality checks → imputation → model training → WQI prediction → SHAP → recommendations → Streamlit app

`Python` `CatBoost` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `Streamlit`

[**Live demo & details →**](https://ameerroshan.me/)
